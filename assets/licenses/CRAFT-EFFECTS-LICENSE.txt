Selected rendering algorithms ported to Go from:

EffectCraft (https://github.com/storytold/effectcraft)
  crates/raster/src/blur.rs: three-box Gaussian kernel selection
  crates/effects/src/color_fx.rs: tint and tritone
  crates/effects/src/misc.rs: posterize, alpha-based single-colour Behind glow
PhotoCraft (https://github.com/storytold/photocraft)
  crates/compose/src/effects.rs: centred gradient geometry and sampling
FilmCraft (https://github.com/storytold/filmcraft)
  crates/render/src/transitions/legacy.rs: dip-to-black and dip-to-white
